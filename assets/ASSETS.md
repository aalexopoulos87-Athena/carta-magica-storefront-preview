- `pokemon-energy.webp`: original yellow/red energy landscape and original distant creature silhouettes.
- `magic-arcane.webp`: original arcane fantasy battlefield and gold spell light.
- `yugioh-ancient.webp`: original purple/gold ancient duel arena.
- `lorcana-storybook.webp`: original gold-ink storybook forest.
- `onepiece-ocean.webp`: original sailing ship and sunset ocean.
- `digimon-digital.webp`: original neon grid and geometric biomechanical guardian.
- `fleshblood-steel.webp`: original medieval steel/crimson battlefield.
- `carta-cardback.webp`: original black/gold embossed card-back-style texture, used as the hero and fallback for other categories.

Banner artwork was created for this prototype. It is not official publisher art, a product photograph, or a copy of a specific card illustration. No named franchise character likenesses or imitation game logos were used. Titles and badges are accessible HTML text layered over the images. Game names identify catalog categories, not an endorsement by a publisher.

All files are local and included in the preview archive. No image hotlinking occurs.
